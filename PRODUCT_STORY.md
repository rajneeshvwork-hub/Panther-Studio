# QuietCue — Product Story

## The problem

Presenters often have the deck, the script, the camera, the microphone, timing pressure, and recording controls competing for attention at the same moment. Screen recorders solve capture, but not presentation delivery.

## Product insight

The audience and the presenter need different interfaces.

The audience needs a clean presentation. The presenter needs private guidance, timing, cues, audio confidence, and control without exposing any of it.

## The product

**QuietCue is a private presentation copilot.**

It supports the full performance loop:

**Setup → Present → Review**

The deck remains the hero. Guidance and setup controls appear only when relevant.

## Core principles

1. **Private by design** — guidance is presenter-only and excluded from the recording canvas.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — users should not face every option at once.
3. **Performance over configuration** — timing, audio, and delivery matter more than decorative settings.
4. **Calm interface** — premium UX comes from hierarchy and restraint.
5. **Flexible sources** — PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF, and images should enter the same workflow.

## Visual direction

QuietCue uses **Porcelain / Ink / Iris**: warm surfaces for preparation, deep ink typography for clarity, iris purple for intentional actions, and a dark theatre around the presentation itself.

## Positioning

> **Present naturally. Stay on track.**

Rather than replacing PowerPoint, Google Slides, or recording tools, QuietCue focuses on the part those products under-serve: the person delivering the presentation.
